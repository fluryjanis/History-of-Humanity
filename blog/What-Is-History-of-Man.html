What Is History of Man?

What History of Humanity is, what “history of man” means, and why preserving the perspective of people living through their own time matters.

Published September 6, 2026 · History of Humanity Archive

History of Humanity is a historical archive focused on preserving how people experienced and understood the periods they lived through. It records not only what happened, but what people believed, expected, feared, created, and considered ordinary at the time.

About this page “History of man” traditionally refers to human history or the history of humanity. History of Humanity is also the name of this particular archival project.

This page explains that distinction and describes what the project means by preserving history from the perspective of people living through it.

1. What is History of Humanity?

History of Humanity is a long-term historical archive focused on preserving how people experienced and understood the periods they lived through.

The project is interested in more than simply recording what happened. It also attempts to preserve what people believed, what they considered normal, what they feared, what they created, and what they expected the future to become.

A historical period can look very different depending on when it is viewed. Someone living through a period does not know how it will eventually turn out. They experience the present without the benefit of hindsight.

History of Humanity exists to preserve some of that perspective while it is still contemporary, creating an archive that future generations can use to better understand not only what happened, but what it was like to live through it.

2. What is history of man?

“History of man” traditionally refers to human history—the story of humanity and its development across time.

Click to reveal more

The phrase history of man is commonly used to refer broadly to the history of humanity. It can include human origins, societies, civilizations, cultures, technology, ideas, conflicts, and everyday life.

In that traditional sense, “history of man” and “human history” are broadly describing the same subject.

History of Humanity uses this broader concept as the foundation for a specific historical archive. The name refers to this project rather than claiming that the phrase “history of man” has only one meaning.

3. Is History of Humanity the same as human history?

Human history is the broad subject; History of Humanity is a particular archive dedicated to documenting it from a specific perspective.

Click to reveal more

Human history is the broad study of humanity across time. It includes everything from early human societies to the modern world.

History of Humanity is the name of this particular project. Its focus is narrower: preserving perspectives, experiences, culture, expectations, and ordinary life from within historical periods.

The project does not claim to redefine human history or replace established historical resources. It is one approach to documenting humanity's past and present.

4. What does History of Humanity document?

The archive documents aspects of human life that help future readers understand what a period was actually like.

Click to reveal more

History of Humanity is interested in both major developments and the ordinary experiences surrounding them.

  • Everyday life
  • Beliefs and ideas
  • Culture
  • Technology
  • Work
  • Entertainment
  • Social changes
  • Fears and concerns
  • Expectations about the future
  • Things people considered ordinary
  • Perspectives that may look different with hindsight

The purpose is not to document every detail of every person's life. Instead, the archive attempts to preserve representative pieces of how people understood and experienced their time.

5. Why preserve perspectives from the present?

People living through a period do not know how that period will eventually turn out.

Click to reveal more

Looking backward gives historians something people living in the present do not have: hindsight.

A future historian may know which technologies succeeded, which predictions failed, and which events became historically important. Someone living through those same events has none of that information.

Their uncertainty is therefore part of the historical record.

A prediction, photograph, recording, or other contemporary source can preserve something that becomes much harder to reconstruct after the outcome is already known.

6. Why preserve ordinary life?

What feels ordinary today may become unfamiliar to future generations.

Click to reveal more

People rarely document everything they consider normal. Everyday habits often seem too obvious to explain.

Yet ordinary life changes constantly. Technologies disappear, occupations change, entertainment evolves, and cultural assumptions shift.

A future reader may understand that something existed without understanding how normal it once felt.

Preserving ordinary life helps provide that missing context.

7. What does “history from the perspective of people living through it” mean?

It means documenting a period before hindsight changes the way that period is understood.

Click to reveal more

A historical event can be described in very different ways depending on when it is being described.

Someone looking backward already knows the outcome. Someone living through the event is dealing with uncertainty, incomplete information, competing possibilities, and expectations about what might happen next.

History of Humanity is particularly interested in preserving that contemporary perspective.

This does not mean that traditional historians ignore contemporary perspectives. Historical research has long used diaries, newspapers, photographs, letters, recordings, interviews, and other contemporary sources.

The distinction is that preserving contemporary perspective is a central purpose of this particular archive.

8. Why document the present?

Every present eventually becomes the past, and things that seem ordinary today can become difficult to reconstruct later.

Click to reveal more

People living in the present naturally tend to think of their own time as normal. It is difficult to know which details of everyday life will eventually matter to historians.

Waiting until a period has passed also means losing some of the perspectives that existed within it.

Documenting the present creates historical material before that distance develops.

9. Who is History of Humanity for?

It is for anyone interested in understanding humanity across time, especially future readers looking back at periods that have already passed.

Click to reveal more

The archive is intended to be useful to people who want to understand how human life and perspectives change over time.

A future reader might already know the major events of a particular period. What they may not know is what people at the time believed, expected, feared, watched, created, or considered completely ordinary.

History of Humanity attempts to preserve some of that context for them.

10. What is the purpose of the History of Humanity archive?

Its purpose is to preserve more than a record of events: it is an attempt to preserve pieces of how humanity understood its own time.

Click to reveal more

History can be viewed through many different perspectives. Major events, political changes, technological developments, and historical outcomes are important parts of the record.

History of Humanity focuses on another part of that record: what it was like to live through a period before knowing how the story would end.

The archive cannot perfectly recreate the past, and it cannot represent every person who lived through it. Its purpose is more modest: to preserve contemporary perspectives and pieces of everyday life that might otherwise become difficult to recover.

The present eventually becomes history. History of Humanity is an attempt to preserve part of that transition.

History is not only what happened. It is also what people living through a period believed, expected, experienced, and considered normal.
