# History of Humanity

### An archive of ordinary human life, preserved year by year.

**History of Humanity** is an attempt to preserve something that traditional history often misses: **ordinary life**.

We remember presidents, wars, inventions, disasters, and other major events. But what was it actually like to be an ordinary person living through those moments?

What did people care about?

What did they watch?

What did they worry about?

What did they believe the future would look like?

What did an ordinary day feel like?

This project is an archive for those things.

---

## The Idea

History is happening right now.

Every year, billions of people experience the world in their own way. Most of those experiences will never be recorded in a history book.

A person living in 2026 might think something is completely normal. Fifty or one hundred years later, that same thing could be fascinating to someone trying to understand what life was really like.

**History of Humanity exists to preserve those ordinary perspectives.**

The goal isn't to predict the future or create a perfect historical record.

It's simply to leave something behind.

---

## What Goes Into the Archive?

Entries can be about almost anything connected to everyday life.

* Daily routines
* School and work
* Technology
* Music and entertainment
* Games
* Food
* Trends
* Hobbies
* Opinions
* Fears and worries
* Things people were excited about
* Major events from an ordinary person's perspective
* Predictions about the future
* Small things that might otherwise be forgotten

An entry doesn't have to be important.

**That's the point.**

---

## Why Year by Year?

The archive is organized around the years people lived through.

Someone in the future should be able to look back and explore:

> **2026 — What was humanity like?**

Then:

> **2027 — What changed?**

And eventually:

> **2050 — What did people from 2026 think the future would become?**

Over enough time, these individual entries could become a snapshot of how ordinary human life changed across generations.

---

## A Time Capsule for Humanity

Think of the project as a continuously growing time capsule.

Not a time capsule containing objects, but one containing **perspectives**.

The internet may eventually contain an enormous amount of information about our time. But information isn't always the same as understanding.

A search engine might tell someone in 2126 what happened in 2026.

This project hopes to help them understand **what it felt like to be there.**

---

## Current Status

This project is currently in its early stages.

The initial website is being built as a simple public archive that can grow over time.

The long-term goal is to make it possible for people to contribute entries and explore the experiences of people from different years.

---

## Philosophy

### Ordinary people matter to history.

You don't need to be famous to have lived through an important period of human history.

You don't need to accomplish something extraordinary for your perspective to be worth preserving.

Sometimes the most interesting historical evidence is simply:

> "This is what life was like for me."

---

## Contributing

As the project develops, contribution features will be added so people can leave their own entries in the archive.

For now, the project is focused on building the foundation.

---

## The Future

Maybe someone will find this project years from now.

Maybe they'll read an entry written by someone who lived in 2026 and think:

> "People really lived like this?"

That's exactly why it exists.

**One day, today will be history.**

Make sure someone remembers what it was like to live it.
